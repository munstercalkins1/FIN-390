# Investment Committee Memo — NIKE, Inc. (NYSE: NKE)

**Decision user:** Buy-side investment committee. **Horizon:** Five-year operating forecast with a terminal-value convention. **Valuation date:** September 10, 2026. **Currency:** USD. **Security:** NIKE Class B common stock. **Share basis:** FY2026 diluted weighted-average shares of 1,481.0m; this is disclosed as a limitation because it is not a period-end fully diluted count.

## Conditional recommendation: Watch / defer

Do not initiate solely from the base case. The model's causal range is **$28.52 to $62.20 per share**, with a **$44.68 base case**, versus the $36.62 same-date market reference. The base scenario assumes that wholesale growth remains constructive, NIKE Direct stabilizes, gross margin gradually improves from FY2026’s 42.9%, and SG&A gains modest operating leverage. Those conditions are plausible but not yet demonstrated strongly enough to justify a standalone initiation.

The favorable case can justify reconsideration only if subsequent disclosures show sustained Direct and digital stabilization, credible margin improvement excluding one-time tariff recovery, and working-capital cash conversion that does not depend on inventory liquidation. The downside case is decision-relevant: ongoing Direct weakness, promotion pressure, weaker Greater China/Converse results, and a higher discount rate can move value materially below the market reference.

## Method and range

The primary method is a five-year FCFF DCF. Forecast operating cash flow is derived from linked revenue, gross-margin, cost, working-capital, capex, and tax drivers. It is discounted at WACC and uses a Gordon-growth terminal value. Cash and short-term investments are added, debt is subtracted, and the result is divided by the disclosed diluted weighted-average shares. The output file reports downside, base, and upside values, a WACC–growth sensitivity, terminal-value share, and a market-implied Direct-growth shift.

The comparable methods are deliberately not mechanically averaged with DCF. The equity-value P/E work admits only lululemon as a positive-GAAP-EPS peer and produces a $15.34 one-peer reference. The separate EV/EBIT cross-check applies lululemon's same-date EV/EBIT to NIKE's tariff-normalized EBIT and produces an $8.50 reference. Both are far below the DCF because lululemon’s observed multiples and business mix differ sharply from NIKE’s global footwear/wholesale economics, while the DCF explicitly values a NIKE margin-and-channel recovery. One peer provides a cautionary market reference, not a sufficient peer range or a reason to average methodologies.

No transaction multiple is used. A control transaction would require evidence of date, control premium, synergies, and a comparable business context; none was qualified here.

## Conditions and monitoring triggers

Revisit toward initiation if all three occur: (1) NIKE Direct and digital sales stop declining on a sustained basis, (2) gross margin improves without relying on temporary tariff recovery or unusually aggressive markdowns, and (3) operating cash conversion improves while inventory days and receivables remain controlled.

Move toward do-not-initiate if Direct/digital weakness persists, Greater China or Converse deterioration worsens, gross margin stays near the downside path because promotions remain necessary, or the market price exceeds the high-case range without evidence that changes the forecast. Re-run the model after each quarterly filing and whenever debt, capital-return policy, or share basis changes materially.

## Limits

Terminal value can represent a substantial share of enterprise value, so this is not a point forecast. The model simplifies many balance-sheet accounts into ratios and uses a historical weighted-average share count. Facts, normalizations, forecasts, and model outputs are distinguished in the source map and assumption register. This is course work, not investment advice.
