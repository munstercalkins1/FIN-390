## Normalization / contamination catalog

| Item | Treatment | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| IEEPA tariff recovery benefit | Do not extrapolate as recurring gross-margin improvement. | The FY2026 10-K describes a tariff-recovery benefit; treating it as a permanent operating rate would overstate normalized margins. |
| Weighted-average diluted shares | Use consistently as the disclosed share basis and disclose its limitation. | It is not necessarily the period-end fully diluted count. |
| Other income and interest | Excluded from FCFF operating cash flow; only income-statement/cash articulation uses other income. | FCFF is pre-financing and based on EBIT after normalized operating tax. |
| Cash and short-term investments | Excluded from enterprise value and added only in the bridge. | Prevents double-counting non-operating financial assets. |
| Debt | Excluded from FCFF and subtracted only in the bridge. | Prevents an FCFF/FCFE basis mismatch. |
