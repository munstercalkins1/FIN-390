## Decision and user

The intended user is an investment committee deciding whether to initiate a position in NIKE, Inc. The decision is whether the estimated intrinsic value provides a sufficient margin of safety versus the September 1, 2026 reference price of $39.06 per share.

## Company and valuation date

NIKE, Inc. (NYSE: NKE). Valuation date: September 1, 2026. Currency: U.S. dollars. Model units: USD millions except per-share amounts.

## Initial thesis

**Initiate - educational base case only.** The model implies a value of $51.53 per share, compared with a $39.06 September 1, 2026 reference price, or approximately 31.9% implied upside. This conclusion is highly dependent on revenue-growth, margin-recovery, WACC, and terminal-growth assumptions; it is not investment advice.

## Unknowns

- Management's forward revenue and margin outlook after FY2026.
- Whether margin recovery can occur at the pace assumed.
- Potential impacts of tariff recoveries, consumer demand, channel mix, Greater China, and Converse.
- A market-calibrated WACC and a peer-comparable valuation cross-check.

## Research plan

1. Review the next NIKE quarterly filing and earnings release for revenue, margin, inventory, and channel trends.
2. Benchmark WACC, growth, and margin assumptions against credible peer and market sources.
3. Build a trading-comparables cross-check.
4. Test value sensitivity to WACC, terminal growth, and margin recovery.
5. Reconcile period-end diluted shares with weighted-average diluted shares before a final committee decision.
