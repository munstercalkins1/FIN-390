# NIKE, Inc. (NYSE: NKE): Company Research Report

**Date:** September 3, 2026  
**Company:** NIKE, Inc.  
**Primary filing:** FY2026 Form 10-K, year ended May 31, 2026

## What NIKE does and how it earns money

NIKE designs, develops, markets, and sells athletic footwear, apparel, equipment, accessories, and services under the NIKE, Jordan, and Converse brands. It earns revenue through two main routes: wholesale sales to external accounts and NIKE Direct, which includes NIKE-owned stores and digital platforms. Nearly all footwear and apparel products are manufactured by independent contractors. [FY2026 Form 10-K, Item 1, pp. 1-2](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/320187/000032018726000088/nke-20260531.htm)

For fiscal 2026, NIKE Brand revenue was $45.2 billion and Converse revenue was $1.2 billion. Within NIKE Brand, wholesale sales were $27.5 billion and NIKE Direct sales were $17.7 billion. [FY2026 Form 10-K, Item 7, pp. 32-33](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/320187/000032018726000088/nke-20260531.htm)

## Recent performance

NIKE reported FY2026 revenue of $46.4 billion, essentially flat from $46.3 billion in FY2025 and below FY2024 revenue of $51.4 billion. On a currency-neutral basis, FY2026 revenue declined 2%. EBIT was $3.85 billion and EBIT margin was 8.3%, compared with 8.2% in FY2025 and 12.7% in FY2024. This shows that revenue stabilized on a reported basis, but profitability remained below the FY2024 level. [FY2026 Form 10-K, Item 7, pp. 31-32](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/320187/000032018726000088/nke-20260531.htm)

Channel results were mixed. FY2026 wholesale revenue increased 6% on a reported basis, while NIKE Direct revenue declined 6%; NIKE Brand Digital sales declined 12%. The company attributed the Direct decline primarily to reduced digital traffic, while wholesale growth was driven by North America and partly offset by Greater China. [FY2026 Form 10-K, Item 7, pp. 32-33](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/320187/000032018726000088/nke-20260531.htm)

The June 30, 2026 full-year results release repeated this mix: wholesale revenue grew while NIKE Direct fell, and Converse revenue declined 31% on a reported basis. This is important because the company is managing a transition in its marketplace mix rather than experiencing broad-based channel growth. [NIKE FY2026 results release, June 30, 2026](https://investors.nike.com/investors/news-events-and-reports/investor-news/investor-news-details/2026/NIKE-Inc--Reports-Fiscal-2026-Fourth-Quarter-and-Full-Year-Results/default.aspx)

## What looks good

NIKE retains a globally recognized portfolio of NIKE, Jordan, and Converse brands, broad product coverage, and a large wholesale-and-direct distribution network. Management states that its strategy is to lead with sport, create innovative products, build consumer connections, and deliver consumer experiences across digital and retail channels. [FY2025 Form 10-K, Item 1, pp. 1-2](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/320187/000032018725000047/nke-20250531.htm)

FY2026 gross margin increased 20 basis points to 42.9%, and total selling and administrative expense was flat at $16.1 billion. These are signs that some cost and gross-margin pressure eased during the year. [FY2026 Form 10-K, Item 7, pp. 32-34](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/320187/000032018726000088/nke-20260531.htm)

North American wholesale growth was a positive offset to weaker regions. Management also says it is investing in product innovation, marketplace elevation, and operating-cost efficiency. [NIKE FY2026 results release, June 30, 2026](https://investors.nike.com/investors/news-events-and-reports/investor-news/investor-news-details/2026/NIKE-Inc--Reports-Fiscal-2026-Fourth-Quarter-and-Full-Year-Results/default.aspx)

## Challenges and risks

The central challenge is that NIKE's reported revenue has not returned to FY2024 levels and currency-neutral FY2026 revenue declined. Management identifies ongoing negative impacts in Greater China and Converse, with its actions expected to continue through fiscal 2027. [FY2026 Form 10-K, Item 7, pp. 30-33](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/320187/000032018726000088/nke-20260531.htm)

NIKE is also exposed to shifts in consumer preferences, design trends, and sports participation. Its 10-K states that it must respond by adjusting product mix and channels, developing new products, and influencing sports and fitness preferences; failure to respond can harm sales and profitability. [FY2026 Form 10-K, Item 1, pp. 2-3](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/320187/000032018726000088/nke-20260531.htm)

The FY2026 gross-margin result included a benefit from the expected recovery of IEEPA tariffs. That benefit should be separated from recurring operating improvement when evaluating the quality of the reported margin result. [FY2026 Form 10-K, Item 7, p. 30](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/320187/000032018726000088/nke-20260531.htm)

## Comparison with prior company statements

In its FY2025 filing, NIKE described a strategy centered on sport, product innovation, consumer connections, and digital and retail experiences. [FY2025 Form 10-K, Item 1, pp. 1-2](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/320187/000032018725000047/nke-20250531.htm) The FY2026 results show that wholesale improved, but Direct and digital sales remained weaker. The earlier strategy has not yet translated into a complete recovery across channels.

FY2024 revenue was $51.4 billion, FY2025 revenue was $46.3 billion, and FY2026 revenue was $46.4 billion. The historical comparison indicates that FY2026 stabilized after the FY2025 decline but did not restore the FY2024 revenue base. [FY2024 Form 10-K, filed July 25, 2024](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/320187/000032018724000044/nke-20240531.htm); [FY2026 Form 10-K, Item 7, p. 32](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/320187/000032018726000088/nke-20260531.htm)

## Outlook and research questions

Management says its portfolio actions are expected to be completed by December 2026, while Greater China and Converse may continue to face negative impacts through fiscal 2027. [FY2026 Form 10-K, Item 7, p. 30](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/320187/000032018726000088/nke-20260531.htm)

The next questions to test are whether NIKE Direct and digital traffic stabilize, whether Greater China improves, whether Converse's reset gains traction, and whether margin improvement continues after isolating the tariff-recovery effect. The next quarterly filing and earnings call are the most relevant near-term sources for these questions. [NIKE Investor Relations events page](https://investors.nike.com/investors/news-events-and-reports/events-and-presentations/event-details/default.aspx)

## Decision: watch-defer

My call is **watch-defer**. NIKE has brand strength, stabilizing reported revenue, stronger wholesale results, and some gross-margin improvement. However, Direct and digital sales are still declining, Greater China and Converse remain unresolved, and part of the FY2026 gross-margin result reflects a tariff-recovery benefit rather than plainly recurring performance.

I would change this call if subsequent filings show sustained currency-neutral growth, a stabilization in NIKE Direct and digital sales, and margin improvement that remains credible after separating unusual tariff-related benefits.
